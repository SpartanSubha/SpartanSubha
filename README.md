## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📊 E-Commerce User Journey Funnel & Revenue Leakage Analysis
> *Where exactly are customers dropping off — and what is it costing?*

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/SpartanSubha/E-Commerce-User-Journey-Funnel-Revenue-Leakage-Analysis)
[![Live Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Dashboard-F2C811?style=flat-square&logo=powerbi&logoColor=black)](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiNzBlZWUwMTMtMDkwMS00ZTZlLWFmNGMtMjk3YTViYjlkNzM0IiwidCI6IjQyZjRkOWVkLTBiYmItNGU5NS1hYmRjLTM5OGM1M2QzNjkxZCJ9)

Analyzed **10,000 users** across the full purchase funnel to identify ₹24.8L in revenue leakage.

**Key Findings:**
- 🚨 Only **10.04%** of visitors completed a purchase
- 💸 **Cart → Checkout** is the biggest leak point (~₹9.64L lost at one stage)
- 📱 **Mobile** converts at just 9.47% | **Organic** at 9.45%
- 🗺️ **South region** leads in geographic revenue leakage

**Built:** Executive KPI Overview · Funnel Diagnostics · Live Revenue Simulator (What-If 1–20%) · Impact vs Effort Recommendation Matrix

`Power BI` `DAX` `What-If Analysis` `Funnel Analytics` `Executive Reporting`

### 📦 Global Freight Cost Intelligence Dashboard
> *A 36% cost surge happened. What did it mean for margins — and what should the business do?*

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/SpartanSubha/Profit-Impact-of-Freight-Cost-Surge)

**Key Findings:**
